The NIST Cybersecurity Framework provides a structured approach for managing cybersecurity risk.

The major cybersecurity functions are Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond and Recover.

Organizations can use these functions to understand cybersecurity risks, implement protective controls, detect suspicious activity, respond to incidents and recover systems after cybersecurity events.

Incident analysis should consider the affected assets, observed behavior, potential impact, detection evidence and appropriate response actions.
